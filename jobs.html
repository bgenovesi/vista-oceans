How it works

Two steps. Basic first, personal second — and private.

  1. 01

    Basic Info

    The first page is just the basics: name you go by, email, phone, roles you want, experience, and your availability.

  2. 02

    Personal & Confidential

    The second page asks for legal name, mailing address, date of birth, and visa / work status — only what payroll and venues legally require.

🔒 Our Privacy Guarantee

Everything on the personal page is confidential. It's used only by Vista Oceans LLC to verify your eligibility to work and to place and pay you for shifts. It's never sold, never shared publicly, and never sent to a venue without your permission for a specific shift or interview. You can ask us to delete it at any time.
