Questions

Everything owners ask us before starting.

How much does it cost?

It depends on scope — a focused diagnostic is a different engagement than a full pre-opening. What we can promise: every proposal has a fixed, agreed-upon fee before work begins, and we prioritize quick wins designed to pay for the engagement itself. Book a free call and we'll give you an honest range.

How quickly will we see results?

Engagements typically target measurable improvement within 30–60 days — usually in labor cost, purchasing, or average check. Deeper cultural and brand changes compound over 6–12 months. We'll tell you upfront what's fast and what's slow.

Do you work with new concepts or existing businesses?

Both. We help new concepts launch right, and we help existing venues perform better or expand. The diagnostic process adapts to either.

Will you work with our existing team, or replace them?

With them — always. Our job is to make your people better, not to bring in outsiders. We train, coach, and build systems your team can run long after we're gone.

Is our information kept confidential?

Completely. We're happy to sign an NDA before we talk numbers. We never share your financials or strategies.

Do you help with hiring and staffing?

Yes. Alongside consulting, we place FOH and BOH talent — from servers and bartenders to executive chefs — plus licensed security guards and door staff. Everyone is sourced and vetted to fit your concept, culture, and standards.

What size businesses do you work with?

Single independent venues through small multi-unit groups — typically owner-operated businesses. Reach out and we'll tell you honestly if we're the right fit.

Your best next step is a conversation.

Thirty minutes, no pitch, no obligation. We'll talk through your biggest challenge — and you'll leave with at least one idea you can use whether we work together or not.
